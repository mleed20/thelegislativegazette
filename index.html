About Us

The Legislative Gazette covers the politics, policies and people of New York State government from the Capitol in Albany. We are a nonpartisan newsroom, published since the 1970s and staffed by a new cohort of reporters, editors, photographers and producers each semester.

We report what lawmakers, agencies and advocates do, and explain how it affects people across the state.
